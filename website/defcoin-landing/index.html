Start Mining

Connect any scrypt miner to our pool:

stratum+tcp://pool.defcoin.fun:3333

Username: your DFC address  •  Password: x

minerd -a scrypt -o stratum+tcp://pool.defcoin.fun:3333 \
  -u YOUR_DFC_ADDRESS -p x
